# Zarvia sales kit: start here

Zarvia is a trade and finance platform. This kit is for the people who sell it. It answers three
questions: what to say, what to show, and what not to promise.

**Version 0.1 (15 Nov 2026), written for Zarvia 2.9.2.0.** It was written from the product as
built. Every click in the demo script is checked again on the demo company once that company is
ready (Phase 2). Until then, rehearse on any company you have access to, and note anything that
reads differently on screen.

## The three rules

1. **Tell one story, not thirty features.** A client remembers a company like theirs getting
   paid faster. They do not remember a list of menus.
2. **Show only what is real.** Everything you show is the product working on the demo company.
   If a client asks for something that is not built, say so. The [do-not-promise list](do-not-promise.md)
   protects you and protects the client.
3. **Let the screen do the talking.** Click, pause, say one sentence about why it matters to
   them. Then stop and let them ask.

## Before you give your first demo

- Read files 1 to 5.
- Give the 30-minute demo to a colleague, using only the script.
- Give it again to someone who plays a difficult client and asks the FAQ questions.
