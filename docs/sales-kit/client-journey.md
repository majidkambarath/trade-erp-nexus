# The client journey: first call to first month

Who does what at each step, and which part of the kit to use. "Product team" means whoever sets
up organisations in the developer console and supports clients after go-live.

## Before they sign

| Step | Who | What happens | Use |
|------|-----|--------------|-----|
| 1. First contact | Sales | The 30-second version, and ask for a meeting | [the-story.md](the-story.md) |
| 2. Discovery | Sales | Twelve questions; decide which demo to give | [discovery-questions.md](discovery-questions.md) |
| 3. Demo | Sales | 10, 30 or 60 minutes on your own demo company | [demo-script.md](demo-script.md), [pre-demo-checklist.md](pre-demo-checklist.md) |
| 4. Same day | Sales | Send the click-through demo link and two or three short videos in their team's language *(coming in phase 3)* | Click-through and videos |
| 5. Second meeting | Sales (+ product team if needed) | The 60-minute version with their accountant | [demo-script.md](demo-script.md) |
| 6. Proposal | Sales | Plan, number of users, go-live date, and **in writing** anything they asked for that is not built | [plans-and-modules.md](plans-and-modules.md), [do-not-promise.md](do-not-promise.md) |

## After they sign: set-up

| Step | Who | What happens |
|------|-----|--------------|
| 7. Create the company | Product team | The organisation is made in the developer console: plan, country, currency, time zone, and the first administrator |
| 8. First sign-in | Client's owner | Zarvia makes them choose their own password straight away |
| 9. Company details | Client (with sales) | Settings > Company: legal name, Arabic name, address, logo, invoice bank details |
| 10. VAT identity | Client | Settings > Business rules > Tax identity: **TRN**, emirate, registered for VAT. Without a TRN an approved sale prints "Invoice", not "Tax invoice" |
| 11. Accounting set-up | Client's accountant (product team helps) | Accounts > Setup: fiscal year, document numbering, tax codes, posting accounts |
| 12. Lists | Client | Customers, suppliers, items (with categories and units) |
| 13. People | Client's owner | People > Users and roles: one account per person, each with the right role; two-step sign-in for anyone who approves money |
| 14. Go-live position | Client's accountant | Accounts > Opening balances: the go-live date, account balances, each customer's and supplier's unpaid invoices, opening stock with cost |
| 15. Email | Product team + client | Settings > Sending, so invoices really reach customers (until then they are only recorded) |

## Learning, without calling support

| Who | Watches | Then |
|-----|---------|------|
| Sales staff | Quote to invoice; Send the invoice and get paid | Raise one practice invoice on the demo company |
| Storekeeper | Buy stock (goods received); Stock and batches | Receive one practice delivery |
| Accountant | Bank reconciliation; VAT return; Month end and reports | Reconcile one practice statement |
| Owner | The two-minute tour; Users, roles and approvals | Approve one invoice from the phone |

Everyone has **Zarvia AI** in the top bar for "how do I…" questions. The videos and the
click-through demos come with phase 3 of the sales-kit plan, in English, Arabic, Hindi and
Malayalam.

## Go-live and the first month

| When | Who | What |
|------|-----|------|
| Go-live day | Client, product team on call | First real invoices; check the dashboard at the end of the day |
| Day 7 | Sales | A call: what is confusing, what is missing. Send questions to the product team |
| First month end | Client's accountant, product team | Close the first month together (Accounts > Setup > Fiscal years) |
| First VAT quarter | Client's accountant | The first VAT return in Zarvia, checked against the accounts |
