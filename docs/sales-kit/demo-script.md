# The demo script

One story in six acts: **Sahara Gate Foodstuff Trading** (see [demo-company.md](demo-company.md))
going through a normal week. It quotes, sells, delivers, invoices, gets paid, buys stock,
reconciles the bank, prepares VAT, and the owner keeps control.

Each step has four parts:

- **Click**: what to do on screen.
- **Say**: one or two sentences, in your own words. Do not read them out.
- **Why it matters**: the client's problem this step answers. Say it only if they look unsure.
- **If they ask**: the likely question and a true answer.

Before you start, run the [pre-demo checklist](pre-demo-checklist.md).

## Pick the version

| Version | Use when | Acts |
|---------|----------|------|
| **10 minutes** | First call, owner is busy, or you are at their office without an appointment | Act 1 (steps 1-2), Act 2 (steps 4-6), Act 4 (step 3), Close |
| **30 minutes** | The normal first demo | All six acts, the steps not marked *60 min* |
| **60 minutes** | Owner plus accountant, or a second meeting | Everything, including the *60 min* steps, plus time for their questions |

The [discovery questions](discovery-questions.md) tell you which acts to stretch and which to cut.

---

## Opening (1 minute)

**Say:** "You told me [their problem from discovery]. I'll show you a foodstuff trader much like
you, Sahara Gate, going through a normal week. Stop me any time."

Then go straight to the screen. No slides.

---

## Act 1. Your business today (3 minutes)

### 1. The dashboard

- **Click:** Home > Dashboard.
- **Say:** "This is Sahara Gate this morning. Not last quarter: today."
- **Why it matters:** problem 3, the owner who only finds out months later.

### 2. Business flow

- **Click:** point at the strip across the top: *bought → in stock → sold → collected*, with what
  customers still owe and what you still owe suppliers.
- **Say:** "This one line is the whole business: what came in, what is sitting in the warehouse,
  what was sold, what is collected and what is still out there."
- **If they ask** "Where do these numbers come from?": "From the invoices and payments themselves.
  Nobody prepares this dashboard; it is worked out from what was entered."

### 3. Where the revenue went

- **Click:** the card under the strip (the ring: goods / running costs / profit). Hover a part.
- **Say:** read the sentence above it out loud: "Of every AED 100 you sell, AED x is profit."
  Then: "Most owners do not know that number."

### 4. One figure, one click

- **Click:** the "customers still owe" tile. It opens the ageing.
- **Say:** "Every figure opens what is behind it. Here is who owes what, and for how long."

### 5. *(60 min)* Finding anything

- **Click:** press **Ctrl+K**, type `stock`, press Enter.
- **Say:** "Nobody has to learn the menus. Type what you want."

---

## Act 2. Order to cash (10 minutes)

This is the heart of the demo. If you only have time for one act, show this one.

### 1. The quotation

- **Click:** Sales > Quotations. Open the quotation to **Royal Feast Catering**.
- **Say:** "Nadia at Royal Feast asked for a price three days ago. This is what went to her: rice
  and oil, valid for two weeks."
- **If they ask** "Can it be emailed from here?": "Today a quotation is printed or downloaded as
  a PDF and marked as sent. Emailing it straight from Zarvia is how the tax invoice works now;
  quotations are next." (See [do-not-promise.md](do-not-promise.md).)

### 2. She says yes

- **Click:** More actions > **Convert to sales order**. Save, then **Confirm** the order.
- **Say:** "She accepted. One click and it is an order. Nothing is typed again."
- **Why it matters:** problem 1, typing everything twice.
- **Say also:** "Confirming the order moves no stock and posts nothing. Only the invoice does that.
  So an order that is cancelled never makes a mess in the books."

### 3. *(60 min)* Delivery

- **Click:** on the order, **Create delivery note**. Show the lines and the "In stock" figure.
- **Say:** "The warehouse picks from this. If the customer takes less, you record what was
  actually delivered, and the invoice follows what was delivered, not what was ordered."

### 4. Yesterday's delivery

- **Click:** Sales > Delivery notes. Open **Al Madina Fresh Supermarket's** note, delivered
  yesterday and signed for by Khalid.
- **Say:** "This went out yesterday. Zarvia knows it is delivered and not invoiced yet, and it
  counts the days you have left to raise the tax invoice."

### 5. One click to an approved invoice

- **Click:** **Create invoice**. Leave **Approve the invoice now** ticked. Confirm.
- **Say:** "One click. Now watch what that one click did."

### 6. What that one click did

- **Click:** open the new invoice, then **Audit trail**.
- **Say:** "The stock went down at what it actually cost us. Al Madina now owes the money. The
  books are posted, sales, cost of sales, VAT. And the VAT is already counted for the return.
  Nobody typed anything twice."
- **Why it matters:** this is the moment problem 1 is solved. Pause here.
- **If they ask** "What if the invoice is wrong?": "A draft can be edited. An approved invoice
  is corrected with a credit note or a return, so the record of what happened stays honest. Your
  accountant will thank you for that."

### 7. Send it

- **Click:** **Send**. Show email (with the PDF and a link) and WhatsApp.
- **Say:** "The customer gets the PDF and a link to their copy, and you can see when they
  opened it. In this demo company nothing actually leaves; that's why it says Recorded only."

### 8. Getting paid

- **Click:** Sales > Invoices. Find **Spice Garden Restaurant's** invoice, 18 days late. Press
  **Receive payment**. The receipt opens with the customer, the invoice and the amount filled in.
  Change the amount to **5,000**, choose **Bank transfer**, and save.
- **Say:** "Spice Garden paid five thousand this morning. Two clicks. The invoice now shows
  partly paid, and what they still owe is right everywhere: the customer page, the ageing, the
  dashboard."
- **If they ask** "And cheques?": "Cheques are in Act 4. Post-dated ones are followed until
  they clear."

### 9. The customer's whole story

- **Click:** Sales > Customers > **Al Madina Fresh Supermarket** > the **Documents** tab.
- **Say:** "When a customer calls and asks 'where is my order?' or 'what do I owe?', the answer
  is on one page: every order, delivery, invoice and payment, in order."

## Act 3. Purchase to pay (6 minutes)

### 1. What to buy

- **Click:** Reports > Stock, the reorder view. **Basmati Rice Premium 5kg** is below its reorder
  level.
- **Say:** "Zarvia tells you what is running low before the warehouse runs out."

### 2. The purchase order

- **Click:** Purchase > Orders. Open the order to **Gulf Grain Importers**, confirmed four days ago.
- **Say:** "This went to Gulf Grain on Tuesday. Today the truck arrived."

### 3. The goods arrive

- **Click:** Purchase > Goods received > **New goods receipt** > **Against a purchase order**.
  Pick the Gulf Grain order. Under *What arrived*, change one line to a smaller quantity and pick
  a reason in **If short, why**. Mark it received.
- **Say:** "The storekeeper records what actually came off the truck. Ten packs short? It is
  written down, with the reason, before anyone pays for them."

### 4. The supplier's bill

- **Click:** from the receipt, **Create invoice**. Type the supplier's own invoice number. Leave
  **Approve the invoice now** ticked.
- **Say:** "The stock is in at the price on the bill, the average cost is updated, and you owe
  Gulf Grain the money. And Zarvia will not let you be billed for more than actually arrived."
- **Why it matters:** paying for goods that never came is a real leak in trading companies.

### 5. *(60 min)* Expiry

- **Click:** Inventory > Batches. The **Pure Ghee** batch that expires within 25 days.
- **Say:** "For food, every batch has its expiry, and sales take the earliest expiry first."

---

## Act 4. Money and tax (6 minutes)

### 1. Cheques

- **Click:** Finance > Cheques. Two customer cheques in hand: **Bright Star** due in two days,
  **Al Reef** in five.
- **Say:** "Nobody has to remember which cheque goes to the bank on Thursday. Zarvia reminds
  you, and if one bounces, the invoices it paid are opened again by themselves."

### 2. The bank

- **Click:** Finance > Reconcile > **Emirates NBD**. Open the **Suggested** lines. Accept one
  match. On the bank-charge line, post the charge straight from the line.
- **Say:** "Yesterday's bank statement is loaded. Zarvia has already matched most lines to the
  receipts and payments in the books. The bank charge nobody entered? Booked from the statement
  line, in one step."
- **If they ask** "How does the statement get in?": "You download it from your bank as Excel,
  CSV or MT940 and load it. There is no live bank connection; see the FAQ."

### 3. The VAT return

- **Click:** Reports > VAT. Open this quarter's draft return.
- **Say:** "This is the VAT 201 return, box by box, built from the approved invoices, bills and
  expenses. Underneath, it is checked against the VAT accounts in the books. When they agree,
  you know the return is right."
- **Click:** open last quarter's return, which is **finalised**.
- **Say:** "Once it is finalised, nothing can be posted into that quarter by mistake. Your
  return and your books can never drift apart."
- **Why it matters:** problem 2. For most owners this is the moment they decide.
- **If they ask** "Does it file with the FTA?": "No. You submit it on the FTA's own portal, using
  these figures, and record the reference here."

### 4. *(60 min)* Closing the month

- **Click:** Accounts > Setup > Fiscal years > the month-end panel. Open the check for this month,
  but do not close it.
- **Say:** "Before a month is closed, Zarvia checks it for you: drafts still open, a bank not
  reconciled, stock that does not agree with the accounts. Once closed, the month is locked."

---

## Act 5. Control (4 minutes)

### 1. Two people for big amounts

- **Click:** Home > Approvals. The **Green Basket** invoice for AED 62,832, *awaiting second
  approver*. Fatima, the accountant, has already approved it once. Approve it.
- **Say:** "Here the owner has decided that anything over 50,000 needs two people. Fatima
  approved it first; now I give the second approval and only then does it post. You choose the
  amount, or switch it off."

### 2. Roles

- **Click:** People > Users and roles. Open the **Sales** role and show the ticks.
- **Say:** "Ravi in sales can quote, sell and see his customers, and cannot see the books or
  delete an approved invoice. Every person gets exactly what their job needs. A role can also have
  an approval limit."
- **If they ask** "What if someone leaves?": "Switch the account off. They are signed out at once,
  everywhere."

### 3. *(60 min)* See it as Ravi

- **Click:** in a private browser window, sign in as Ravi.
- **Say:** "This is what Ravi sees. No Finance, no Accounts. The server enforces it, not just
  the menu."

### 4. Who did what

- **Click:** go back to the invoice from Act 2 > **Audit trail** > its activity.
- **Say:** "Who made it, who changed it, who approved it, when. For every invoice and every
  voucher."

### 5. *(60 min)* Credit limits

- **Click:** start a sales invoice for **Coral Bay Grocery** for about 8,000 and try to approve it.
- **Say:** "Coral Bay is close to its credit limit, so Zarvia warns before you sell them more.
  Only someone you allow can go past it."

### 6. *(60 min)* Sign-in security

- **Click:** Settings > Security.
- **Say:** "Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app, for everyone or for the people you
  choose. A forgotten password is reset by email."

---

## Act 6. Reports, phone and help (4 minutes)

### 1. The statements

- **Click:** Reports > Statements. Profit and loss, then the balance sheet.
- **Say:** "Profit and loss with gross profit, and the balance sheet against last year. Your
  accountant reads these on the day the month ends, not three weeks later."

### 2. Who owes what

- **Click:** Reports > Ageing. Spice Garden is still in the late column, with less owed since
  this morning.
- **Say:** "Who is late, by how much, for how long. Your collections list for the week."

### 3. A statement for the customer

- **Click:** Reports > Account statement. Pick Spice Garden. **Download PDF**.
- **Say:** "The customer statement, on your letterhead, ready to send."

### 4. On the phone

- **Click:** open Zarvia on your phone (it is installed like an app). Show the dashboard and the
  approvals.
- **Say:** "The owner approves from the phone, wherever they are. The same system, made for the
  small screen."

### 5. Help without calling anyone

- **Click:** the Zarvia AI button in the top bar. Ask: *How do I record a post-dated cheque?*
- **Say:** "Your team asks Zarvia how to do something and gets the steps, with a button to
  open the right screen. It answers from the product's own help, and it never sees your figures."

---

## Close (2 minutes)

**Say:** "So: the invoice is typed once and everything follows. The VAT return builds itself
and is checked against the books. And you can see the business today, with two people
approving the big amounts."

**Ask:** "Which of those would make the biggest difference to you this quarter?"

Then agree the next step, in this order of preference:

1. A second meeting with their accountant, the 60-minute version.
2. Send them the click-through demo link and the short videos, in their team's language, so
   they can show their own people.
3. A proposal with the plan that fits (see [plans-and-modules.md](plans-and-modules.md)).

---

## If something goes wrong on screen

- Do not debug in front of the client. Say "let me show you that another way" and move to the
  next step.
- Write down what you clicked and what happened, with the time, and send it to the product team
  the same day.
- If the app is slow to open, the server was asleep. That is the hosting plan and it is why the
  checklist says to open the app three minutes early.
