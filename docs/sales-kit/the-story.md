# The story

## In one sentence

**Zarvia runs a trading company's buying, selling, stock and books in one place, so an invoice is
typed once and everything else follows: stock, the customer's balance, the accounts and the VAT
return.**

## Who it is for

Trading companies in the UAE with 3 to 50 people in the office: distributors, wholesalers,
importers, food and general traders. They buy from suppliers, hold stock, sell on credit, collect
by cheque and bank transfer, and file a VAT return every quarter.

The first demo company is a foodstuff trader. Its rice, sugar, oil and flour are things every UAE
client recognises.

## The three problems it solves

Say these in the client's words, not ours. When a client nods at one of them, that is the act to
spend time on.

### 1. "Our numbers live in five places."

Sales on Excel, the books in Tally, stock on a sheet in the warehouse, approvals on WhatsApp,
invoices in a book. Everything is typed twice and never quite agrees.

**What Zarvia does:** when an invoice is approved, the stock goes down at its real cost, the
customer owes the money, the accounts are posted and the VAT is counted. Nobody types it again.
*(Show: Act 2.)*

### 2. "VAT return week is the worst week of the quarter."

Someone collects every invoice, adds up the VAT and hopes it agrees with the accounts.

**What Zarvia does:** it builds the VAT 201 return box by box from the approved documents, and
checks it against the VAT accounts in the books. Once a return is finalised, nothing can be
posted into that period by mistake.
*(Show: Act 4.)*

### 3. "I only know how we did when the accountant tells me, months later."

What customers owe, what is in stock, the real margin, whether the cash is coming in: the owner
cannot see any of it today.

**What Zarvia does:** the dashboard shows it now. It shows what customers owe and what you owe
suppliers, stock at cost, gross margin, and how many days the cash is tied up. Every figure opens
the document behind it.
*(Show: Act 1 and Act 6.)*

## And one thing owners always care about: control

Who approved this discount? Who deleted that invoice? Zarvia knows. Each person gets a role, an
approval limit and, when you want it, a second approver for large amounts. Every document has an
audit trail that shows who made it, changed it and approved it, and what it posted.
*(Show: Act 5.)*

## The 30-second version (say this when asked "so what is it?")

> "Zarvia is one system for buying, selling, stock and accounts, built for trading companies in
> the UAE. You enter an invoice once, and the stock, the customer's balance, the books and your
> VAT return all update themselves. Owners use the dashboard to see what they are owed and what
> they are making, today, not at the end of the quarter. Can I show you a company like yours?"

## Who it is NOT for (yet). Be honest; it saves everyone's time

- A shop that needs a point-of-sale till with a cash drawer and receipt printer.
- A company that holds stock in several warehouses and needs to know the quantity in each.
  Zarvia keeps one stock figure per item for the whole company.
- An exporter that invoices mainly in foreign currency. Receipts and payments can be in a foreign
  currency, but an invoice is in the company's own currency.
- A company that must send e-invoices through an accredited provider TODAY.
