# Do not promise

Things Zarvia does **not** do today (2.9.2.0, 15 Nov 2026). If a client asks, say "not today",
then say what Zarvia does instead (the right-hand column). Never give a date for any of these.
If a client needs one of them to sign, write it down and pass it to the product team; they decide
what is built and when.

The list changes with every release. Check it before each first meeting.

## Documents and selling

| Not built | Say instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| Emailing a **quotation, delivery note or statement** straight from Zarvia | The tax invoice is emailed with its PDF and a link. The others are downloaded as PDF and sent by you |
| **Invoices in a foreign currency** | Receipts and payments can be in a foreign currency at a stated rate; the invoice is in AED |
| **A different invoice layout**, or **fields of your own** on a document | Logo, letterhead, Arabic name, bank details, terms and numbering are yours to set |
| **Recurring invoices** (a monthly subscription raised by itself) | Each invoice is raised by a person |
| A **point-of-sale till** (cash drawer, receipt printer) | Walk-in cash sales are an ordinary invoice to a cash customer |
| **Price lists** per customer or per customer group | The price is typed or picked on the line |

## Stock

| Not built | Say instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| **More than one warehouse or location**, or stock **per branch** | One stock figure per item for the whole company |
| **Landed cost** (freight and duty added to the item's cost) | Freight on a purchase is booked as an expense |
| **Buying in one unit and selling in another** (cartons in, pieces out) | Ask the product team before answering; do not demo it |

## Tax and accounts

| Not built | Say instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| **E-invoicing through an accredited service provider** (the UAE mandate) | The PINT AE format is built and tested against a test service; the live connection is not. No date |
| **Filing the VAT return** with the FTA from Zarvia | Zarvia prepares the return box by box; you file on the FTA portal |
| **Corporate tax** calculation | The statements give the accountant the figures |
| VAT on **imports through customs** (boxes 6 and 7 of the VAT 201) | Ask the product team |
| A **live connection to the bank** (bank feeds) | Download the statement from the bank and load it (Excel, CSV, MT940) |
| **Budgets** and **cost centres** | Ask the product team |
| A **bank account in a foreign currency** | Ask the product team |

## Platform and data

| Not built | Say instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Importing customers, suppliers, items or opening balances from a spreadsheet** | They are entered on screen; for long lists, the product team decides how they are loaded |
| A **link to Tally** or another system, or an **API** for other software | Zarvia replaces those systems; it does not sync with them |
| Screens in **Arabic** (or Hindi, Malayalam) | Screens are in English. Guides and videos come in English, Arabic, Hindi and Malayalam |
| Working **offline** | It needs internet |
| A **hosting country**, **backup schedule** or **uptime guarantee** | The product team confirms these for each contract |

## Control

| Not built | Say instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| Approval chains of **more than two people** | Up to two approvers, with a limit per role |
| A different approval limit **per person** (rather than per role) | Make a role for that person |
| Fingerprint or passkey sign-in | Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app |

## Handle with care in a demo

- **A discount on the whole invoice** (not on a line). How it treats VAT is still being decided
  by the product team. In a demo, give discounts on the line.
- **Speed.** The demo server sleeps when nobody uses it and takes up to half a minute to wake.
  Do not say "this is how fast it will be for you". The client's own hosting is agreed in the
  contract.
- **Anything you have not seen work on the demo company.** If you have not clicked it yourself,
  do not show it for the first time in front of a client.
