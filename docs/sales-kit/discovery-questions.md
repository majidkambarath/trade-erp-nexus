# Discovery: ask before you demo

Ten minutes of questions before the demo (or on the phone the day before) decide which acts of
the [demo script](demo-script.md) to stretch and which to cut. Write the answers down; they go
into the proposal and into the set-up.

## The questions

| # | Ask | Listen for | Then |
|---|-----|-----------|------|
| 1 | "What do you sell, and who to?" | Goods or services; supermarkets, restaurants, other traders | If it is mainly a shop counter or a factory, see "Not a fit" below |
| 2 | "What do you use today for invoices, stock and accounts?" | Excel, Tally, a paper book, another system | Problem 1. Spend time on Act 2 |
| 3 | "How many people would use it, and what do they do?" | Sales, store, accounts, the owner | Sizes the plan; Act 5 if there are more than three |
| 4 | "Do you have more than one branch or warehouse?" | Branches: Premium. Warehouses: not a fit | Say honestly that stock is one figure for the company |
| 5 | "Are you registered for VAT? Who prepares the return?" | An outside accountant who struggles every quarter | Problem 2. Act 4 is the closing act |
| 6 | "How do customers pay you? Cheques? Cards?" | Post-dated cheques, card terminals | Act 4, cheques and the bank |
| 7 | "Do you sell on credit? Does anyone go past their limit?" | Late payers, a collections problem | Act 2 step 8, Act 5 credit limits, Act 6 ageing |
| 8 | "Do you have batches or expiry dates?" | Food, medicine, cosmetics | Act 3 step 5 |
| 9 | "Who approves big orders or payments today?" | "The owner, on WhatsApp" | Act 5 |
| 10 | "What would you want to know every morning that you don't know today?" | Cash, what is owed, margin | Act 1, and say their words back to them in the close |
| 11 | "Do you invoice in a foreign currency? Do you need e-invoicing now?" | Yes to either | Check [do-not-promise.md](do-not-promise.md) before going further |
| 12 | "When do you want to start using it, and why then?" | A new year, a new branch, an audit | The date in the proposal |

## Which demo to give

- **Owner alone, busy:** the 10-minute version, built round their answer to question 10.
- **Owner and accountant:** the 60-minute version. Spend the time on Acts 4 and 6.
- **Operations manager / storekeeper:** Acts 2 and 3 in full, short on the rest.

## Not a fit (yet): say so early

- Needs a point-of-sale till, stock per warehouse, invoices in a foreign currency, or live
  e-invoicing through a provider **now**. See [the-story.md](the-story.md).
- Manufactures products, or runs a garage: there are separate modules for these; book a demo with
  the product team rather than giving the trading demo.
